SerialCraft Lab

AI memory for long-running stories.

SerialCraft Lab is an AI-powered writing assistant designed for serialized fiction and web novel creators.

Long-running stories quickly become difficult to manage. Characters change, plot threads accumulate, and small continuity errors become harder to catch as a series grows.

What we're building

  • Character and relationship tracking
  • Story continuity checking
  • Unresolved plot-thread detection
  • Chapter summaries and long-term story memory
  • Context retrieval for upcoming chapters

Our Goal

Help writers spend less time searching old chapters and more time writing new ones.

Current Status

SerialCraft Lab is currently in early development.

We are building the first prototype using large language models to analyze story context and maintain structured memory across long-form serialized fiction.
